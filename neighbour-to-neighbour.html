Neighbour to Neighbour

Hyperlocal Tool-Lending & Community Trust Platform connecting neighbors to share household items, eliminate wasteful duplicate purchases, and foster local trust.

Role Solo UI/UX Designer
Category Community Tech & Trust
Tool Figma
Status Interactive Prototype
01. The Problem

Household tools sit idle 95% of the time while neighbors buy duplicates.

Most households own expensive tools (drills, ladders, pressure washers) that are used only a few times per year.

Meanwhile, nearby neighbors buy exact duplicate items for one-off tasks. Informal borrowing currently relies on personal acquaintances or unorganized WhatsApp groups with zero tracking or accountability, creating awkwardness around item damage or delayed returns.

02. Core Objectives

Three pillars of the Neighbour to Neighbour ecosystem.

01
Hyperlocal Item Discovery. Allow users to search available tools and equipment within a 2-km radius with real-time availability status.
02
Verified Borrowing & Return Handoff. Seamless QR-code verification during item pick-up and return to guarantee accountability and peace of mind.
03
Frictionless Community Requests. Post quick help or item requests to verified neighbors in seconds without social anxiety.

04. Key Design Decisions

Designing for trust and local accountability.

Decision 01

Proximity-Based Map & List Toggle

Enabled users to switch effortlessly between map view (seeing nearby tools within walking distance) and filtered category lists.

Decision 02

Trust Score & Damage Coverage

Integrated community trust badges and item return reminders so lenders feel 100% confident sharing valuable equipment.

05. Reflection & Takeaways

Key insights gained from designing Neighbour to Neighbour.

  • Trust is the Core Product: Peer-to-peer sharing platforms succeed when safety and accountability are built into every interaction.
  • Micro-Location Matters: Keeping searches focused within a tight neighborhood radius increases fulfillment speed and community bonding.
  • Clear Borrower Contracts: Transparent terms upfront eliminate awkwardness and build long-term platform participation.
