Butterfingers

Artisan Home Bakery Mobile App & Scheduled Delivery Flow featuring customized cake builders, transparent pricing, and real-time order tracking.

Role Solo UI/UX Designer
Category Mobile E-Commerce
Tool Figma
Status Interactive Prototype
01. The Problem

Ordering custom baked goods shouldn't feel like endless back-and-forth messages.

Home bakeries offer unmatched quality, but their order intake is often fragmented.

Custom cake ordering currently relies heavily on unorganized messaging channels (WhatsApp, Instagram DMs). Customers struggle to specify flavors, dietary options, and delivery time slots, while bakers spend hours clarifying details and confirming payments manually.

02. Core Objectives

Three pillars of the Butterfingers experience.

01
Interactive Visual Cake Customizer. Allow customers to pick cake tiers, flavors, frostings, and custom messages with instant visual previews.
02
Guaranteed Delivery Slots. Clear calendar scheduling for fresh delivery times, eliminating pickup confusion for celebrations.
03
Real-Time Preparation Status. Track order progress from baking in the oven to final decoration and out-for-delivery updates.

04. Key Design Decisions

Crafting delight while reducing checkout friction.

Decision 01

Step-by-Step Customizer Progress

Broke complex customization into a 4-step guided wizard (Base → Filling → Toppers → Inscription) to prevent choice overload.

Decision 02

Upfront Price Transparency

Live order total updates instantly as options are selected, so customers never face unexpected price surprises at checkout.

05. Reflection & Takeaways

Key insights gained from building Butterfingers.

  • Sensory UI Design: Warm color palettes and rich micro-typography elevate appetite appeal and brand trust.
  • Streamlined Checkout: Reducing input fields during address selection boosted checkout speed significantly.
  • Empowering Home Creators: Standardized order intake frees home bakers to focus on baking rather than admin messaging.
